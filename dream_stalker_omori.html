COMMANDS & ADMIN CONTROLS: CLICK COMMAND TO SIMULATE

Gunakan perintah konsol berikut di server Fabric 1.21.8 kamu, atau klik tombol [ EXECUTE ] untuk mencoba efeknya langsung di halaman ini:

/stalker summon
Summons the Stalker entity near a specified player.
/stalker fear <0-100>
Sets the current player fear level manually (0 to 100 scale).
/stalker dream <map>
Teleports target player into a chosen Dreamspace (mossy_rooms, motel_treats, lone_theatre).
/stalker shift
Triggers an immediate reality shift and illusion cycle.
